About Us

3D4E is a multi-collegiate organization of students interested in 3D printing and its applications.

We provide 3D printing expertise & engineering resources to all UCLA and her surrounding community. We also enable you to bring your transformative ideas to life, with a little elbow grease and a helping hand! We pride ourselves on our openness, our response times, our material selections, and our printer infrastructure to make sure the only question you have about your projects will be how soon can I get it.

We offer printing resources in our lab (Boelter 6514) and technical expertise with our board members! Come say hi, we always have someone between 12-5 PM. It's a space for any student to print, study or just hang out (˶ᵔヮᵔ˶)

Additionally, we:

  • host weekly project hours to collaborate on multidisciplinary projects
  • offer workshops on 3D modeling/printing software and skills
  • host social events to encourage bonding and teamwork
  • organize outreach events for local middle- and high-school students
  • connect with companies and alumni to bring opportunities to students

Our Arsenal

YVELTAL
Voron 2.4
Type: FDM | Vol: 350x350x330mm
Yveltal is one of the largest 3D printers at UCLA accessible to students, built by hand from prior members.
Supported Materials:
ABS PETG TPU PLA
XERNEAS
Sovol SV08
Type: FDM | Vol: 350x350x345mm
Xerneas' design is heavily inspired by the Voron 2.4. Our model is enclosed, and has better part cooling than default.
Supported Materials:
ABS PETG TPU PLA
PANCHAM
Bambu Labs P1P
Type: FDM | Vol: 256x256x256 mm
An unenclosed, very reliable, and speedy printer, but a little finicky as it does not live within our opensource ecosystem.
Supported Materials:
PETG TPU PLA
ODDISH
Ender 3
Type: FDM | Vol: 220x220x250mm
Relic from a past age. Makes one feel nostalgic almost. Kept for when print volume is necessary during peaks. We are currently trying to repurpose this into a Duender.
Supported Materials:
PETG PLA
GENGAR
Ender 3 Pro
Type: FDM | Vol: 220x220x250mm
Another relic from a past age, this time in purple. Kept for when print volume is necessary during peaks. We are currently trying to repurpose this into a Duender.
Supported Materials:
PETG PLA
LITTEN
Prusa Mk. 2
Type: FDM | Vol: 250x210x200mm
Final relic from a past age, in beautiful Prusa Orange. Kept for when print volume is necessary during peaks and when other printers require emergency parts.
Supported Materials:
PETG PLA
ROTOM
Heygears Reflex
Type: SLA | Vol: 222x122x228mm
UV Printer accessible to only Board Members to print on due to safety. High resolution small models work best with it.
Supported Materials:
UV curable Resin (BYO)
