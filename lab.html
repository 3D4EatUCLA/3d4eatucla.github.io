Our Lab (Boelter 6514)

This is where a lot of people within our organization work, hang out, and relax.

We have two areas: a workshop with all our printers, tools, paint, etc... and our lounge area with music, board games, and tea!

Our lab space is free to access, free to use, and has ample space for anyone to work in.

We have access to paints, sandpaper, printers, and other tools for all your project needs. And we also have a lounge next to our workshop, where we hang out, host events, and chill after a long day's work.

For safety purposes, do not bring items from one room to another.

Our Arsenal

YVELTAL
Voron 2.4
Type: FDM | Vol: 350x350x330mm
Yveltal is one of the largest 3D printers at UCLA accessible to students, built by hand from prior members.
Supported Materials:
ABS PETG TPU PLA
XERNEAS
Sovol SV08
Type: FDM | Vol: 350x350x345mm
Xerneas' design is heavily inspired by the Voron 2.4. Our model is enclosed, and has better part cooling than default.
Supported Materials:
ABS PETG TPU PLA
PANCHAM
Bambu Labs P1P
Type: FDM | Vol: 256x256x256 mm
An unenclosed, very reliable, and speedy printer, but a little finicky as it does not live within our opensource ecosystem.
Supported Materials:
PETG TPU PLA
ODDISH
Ender 3
Type: FDM | Vol: 220x220x250mm
Relic from a past age. Makes one feel nostalgic almost. Kept for when print volume is necessary during peaks. We are currently trying to repurpose this into a Duender.
Supported Materials:
PETG PLA
GENGAR
Ender 3 Pro
Type: FDM | Vol: 220x220x250mm
Another relic from a past age, this time in purple. Kept for when print volume is necessary during peaks. We are currently trying to repurpose this into a Duender.
Supported Materials:
PETG PLA
LITTEN
Prusa Mk. 2
Type: FDM | Vol: 250x210x200mm
Final relic from a past age, in beautiful Prusa Orange. Kept for when print volume is necessary during peaks and when other printers require emergency parts.
Supported Materials:
PETG PLA
ROTOM
Heygears Reflex
Type: SLA | Vol: 222x122x228mm
UV Printer accessible to only Board Members to print on due to safety. High resolution small models work best with it.
Supported Materials:
UV curable Resin (BYO)
